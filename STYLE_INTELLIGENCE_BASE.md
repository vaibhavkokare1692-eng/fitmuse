## Rule Set: Masculine Old Money / Quiet Luxury Date System

### Core style philosophy

Masculine Old Money / Quiet Luxury should feel understated, calm, and refined rather than flashy or trend-chasing. The overall signal should be confidence, credibility, restraint, and ease.

### Quiet luxury / stealth wealth principles

- Prioritize substance over hype.
- Choose timeless pieces over trend-driven statement items.
- Use quality-looking fabrics, clean shapes, and quiet color combinations.
- Let fit, texture, and restraint carry the outfit.

### No-logo rule

- Avoid visible logos and loud branding.
- The outfit should feel expensive because of proportion, texture, and coordination, not because the brand name is obvious.

### Fewer, better items principle

- Use fewer items, but make each one count.
- Strong core pieces matter more than extra layers or accessories.
- For lower budgets, a clean 3-piece look is better than forcing a weak 5-piece look.

### Natural fabric preference

Prioritize fabrics that look grounded and premium:

- oxford cotton
- merino
- cashmere
- wool flannel
- suede
- linen
- cotton

Avoid shiny synthetics or anything that looks loud, plastic, or overly polished.

### Fit and tailoring priority

- Fit matters more than brand.
- Clothes should skim the body without clinging or hanging loosely.
- Trousers should create a clean vertical line.
- Jackets and outer layers should sit cleanly through the shoulder.
- Tailoring can make budget clothes look more premium.

### Date-night softness and approachability

For date settings, the look should feel polished but approachable.

- Avoid stiffness
- Avoid looking over-formal
- Use softness through knitwear, texture, rich neutrals, and relaxed polish
- Aim for calm confidence rather than business-formal distance

## Outfit Formulas

### 1. Oxford shirt + tailored trousers + loafers

- Best occasion: dinner date, smart-casual evening, first date, upscale casual venue
- Colors: off-white or cream shirt, charcoal or camel trousers, brown or burgundy loafers
- Fit notes: slim or regular through the shirt, clean straight leg or slim-straight trouser
- Why it works: simple, timeless, and polished without looking too formal
- Budget-friendly version: oxford shirt + chinos + penny loafers or clean minimal loafers

### 2. Knitted polo + tailored or flannel trousers + loafers

- Best occasion: dinner date, lounge date, evening drinks
- Colors: cream, forest green, navy, burgundy with stone, charcoal, or camel trousers
- Fit notes: polo should skim the torso; trousers should fall cleanly without stacking
- Why it works: knit texture adds softness and approachability while staying refined
- Budget-friendly version: fine-knit polo + chinos + loafers

### 3. Quarter-zip + Oxford shirt + chinos

- Best occasion: cooler date night, coffee-to-dinner, casual refined settings
- Colors: camel, navy, stone, cream, charcoal
- Fit notes: quarter-zip should layer cleanly over the shirt without bulk
- Why it works: creates quiet structure and layered polish without stiffness
- Budget-friendly version: cotton quarter-zip + white oxford + beige chinos

### 4. Textured cardigan + button-down shirt + dark trousers

- Best occasion: intimate date, indoor evening setting, relaxed upscale venue
- Colors: oatmeal, taupe, forest green, charcoal, off-white
- Fit notes: cardigan should be soft but not oversized; shirt collar should sit cleanly
- Why it works: texture adds warmth and depth, making the outfit feel refined and approachable
- Budget-friendly version: textured cotton cardigan + oxford shirt + dark chinos

### 5. Suede or textured layer + tailored trousers + Chelsea boots

- Best occasion: evening date, cool-weather dinner, dressed-up smart casual
- Colors: dark brown, camel, charcoal, stone, forest green
- Fit notes: outer layer should fit close through shoulder and torso; boots should stay sleek
- Why it works: suede and leather texture elevate the outfit without using loud details
- Budget-friendly version: faux-suede style jacket with clean Chelsea-inspired boots and tailored chinos

### 6. Cream crewneck + dark or earth-tone trousers + minimal leather sneakers

- Best occasion: casual date, daytime date, gallery or cafe setting
- Colors: cream, off-white, chocolate, stone, deep olive, navy
- Fit notes: knit should skim the body; trousers should stay clean at the ankle
- Why it works: relaxed but premium, especially when the sneaker stays minimal
- Budget-friendly version: cotton crewneck + beige chinos + minimal white sneakers

### 7. Navy blazer + off-white Oxford shirt + camel or charcoal trousers + loafers

- Best occasion: more elevated date, smart dinner, stylish event-adjacent outing
- Colors: navy, off-white, camel, charcoal, brown
- Fit notes: unstructured blazer preferred; avoid a stiff corporate fit
- Why it works: keeps the look polished, masculine, and timeless without looking like officewear
- Budget-friendly version: soft navy blazer + oxford shirt + chinos + loafers

## Product Selection Rules

- Recommend: soft white, cream, off-white, pale blue, muted stripe

- Price target: entry to mid tier, especially if fit is strong

- Materials: oxford cotton, cotton-linen blends
- Colors: white, off-white, pale blue, cream

- Recommend: fine-knit, textured knit, muted tones

## Color Rules

- Core palette: cream, navy, camel, forest green, burgundy, charcoal, stone, white/off-white
- Use cream or navy as an anchor color
- Keep outfits to 2–3 main colors
- Use mostly neutrals with one muted accent
- For brown or dark skin tones, ivory or off-white can work better than harsh bright white
- Forest green, deep maroon, rust, navy, camel, and off-white are strong premium tones
- Avoid neon, royal blue, electric colors, and overly saturated tones

### Skin-tone note

Do not assume skin tone from nationality. Use skin-tone color rules only if the user provides that preference or asks for it.

---

## Fit and Proportion Rules

- Clothing should skim the body, not cling or bag
- Avoid skinny and extra-slim fits for Old Money looks
- Trousers should have clean vertical lines and should not stack heavily at the ankle
- Broad shoulders plus lean legs: prefer regular or slim-straight pants, not skinny pants
- Midsection or belly: prioritize vertical lines, monochrome color families, structured layers
- Slim frame: use textured knitwear and layered depth
- Jacket shoulders should align with the natural shoulder

---

## Budget Rules for $100-$200

- Prioritize core pieces first
- Do not force a full 5-piece outfit if budget is low
- Allow a 3-piece outfit: top + bottom + shoes
- Add accessory only if budget allows
- Add outerwear only if budget allows
- Tailoring can make budget clothes look more premium
- Show budget labels:
  - Within budget
  - Near budget
  - Over budget but strong style match
- Budget-conscious users should not be pushed only toward luxury-tier products

## Rule Set: Smart Casual / Office Style System

- Polished, but not overly formal

- Workplace-appropriate silhouettes first

- Sharp shoes and clean layers do a lot of the styling work
- Low visual noise reads more premium and more credible
- Budget realism matters more than office fantasy styling
- Office dress-code flexibility should be respected instead of assumed

- Best occasion: office day, meeting, interview, business-casual commute
- Recommended colors: white/off-white, soft blue, navy, charcoal
- Fit notes: clean shoulder, straight or slim-straight trouser, no tight shirt pull
- Why it works: this is the most dependable office-ready baseline
- Budget-friendly version: wrinkle-resistant shirt + clean chinos + soft loafers

##### 2. Knit polo + chinos + clean sneakers or loafers

- Best occasion: casual office, startup office, warmer workday
- Recommended colors: navy, beige, olive, camel, cream
- Fit notes: polo should skim the torso, chinos should stay clean through the leg
- Why it works: relaxed polish without looking underdressed
- Budget-friendly version: cotton knit polo + tapered chinos + clean court sneaker

##### 3. Quarter-zip + button-down + tailored trousers

- Best occasion: cooler office, office travel day, meeting-heavy day
- Recommended colors: charcoal, navy, cream, soft blue
- Fit notes: neat layering only, no bulky athletic zip
- Why it works: gives comfort and warmth while staying workplace-appropriate
- Budget-friendly version: cotton quarter-zip + basic button-up + office trouser

##### 4. Unstructured blazer + T-shirt or knit + chinos

- Best occasion: business casual, founder office, presentation day
- Recommended colors: navy, charcoal, camel, cream
- Fit notes: one structured layer, one soft base, trousers should stay tidy
- Why it works: smart without becoming stiff
- Budget-friendly version: soft blazer + clean tee + chinos + loafers

##### 5. Merino sweater + trousers + Chelsea boots

- Best occasion: cooler office, city office, polished commute day
- Recommended colors: charcoal, navy, camel, olive
- Fit notes: smooth torso line, trousers should not stack heavily
- Why it works: quiet and refined with long-wear comfort
- Budget-friendly version: cotton-merino sweater + tailored chinos + sleek boots

### E. Fit and silhouette rules

- Structured, but comfortable
- Not too tight
- Not too oversized unless balanced
- Trousers should fall cleanly
- Tops should tuck or half-tuck cleanly when appropriate
- Petite users often benefit from high-rise bottoms and pointed shoes
- Tall users can use longer layers and wide-leg trousers more easily
- Relaxed-fit users should still keep one structured element

### F. Budget rules

#### Under $100

- Prioritize top + bottom + shoes
- Use minimal accessories only if budget allows
- Avoid forcing blazer or outerwear

#### $100-$200

- Build a complete affordable smart-casual look
- Top + bottom + shoes + small accessory is realistic

#### $200-$350

- Allow blazer, cardigan, structured bag, or better shoes

#### $350+

- Allow premium blazer, coat, structured bag, and stronger wool, silk, or merino pieces

- clubwear tops
- overly shiny synthetic fabrics
- loud logos
- clothes that are too tight or too revealing for office context
- overly formal suits when the user selected smart casual

## Rule Set: Feminine Clean / Minimal / Quiet Luxury Date & Everyday System

### A. Key principles

#### Intentional simplicity

- Start with fewer, stronger pieces.
- Let proportion, material, and color discipline do most of the work.
- Avoid adding items just to make the outfit feel "styled."

#### Quality over quantity

- A smaller number of better-looking pieces usually reads more premium than many trend-led pieces.
- The outfit should feel calm, selective, and repeatable.

#### Structure vs softness

- The strongest feminine clean looks balance one structured signal with one softer signal.
- Examples:
  - structured blazer + fluid dress
  - tailored trousers + silk shirt
  - fitted knit + relaxed denim

#### Subtle luxury through fabric and fit

- Quiet luxury should come from material, drape, tailoring, and restraint.
- Natural fibers and clean construction matter more than overt branding.

#### Third-piece rule

- A blazer, cardigan, sweater, scarf, or structured bag can make a simple outfit feel finished.
- The third piece should elevate the look, not overwhelm it.

#### Clean Girl vs Quiet Luxury distinction

- Clean Girl is often fresher, more youthful, and wellness-coded.
- Quiet Luxury is more mature, fabric-focused, and tailoring-driven.
- They overlap, but they are not identical.

### B. Outfit formulas

#### 1. Basic white T-shirt + high-rise straight-leg jeans + leather loafers + gold hoops

- Best occasion: daily wear, casual date, coffee run, low-key dinner
- Colors: white, blue denim, camel, cream, gold
- Fit notes: the tee should sit cleanly at the shoulder; denim should be high-rise and straight rather than sloppy
- Why it works: simple, repeatable, flattering, and easy to make polished
- Budget-friendly version: cotton white tee + straight denim + loafers or pointed flats

#### 2. Oversized blazer as dress + thin belt + knee-high boots

- Best occasion: date night, dinner, event-adjacent evening
- Colors: black, charcoal, camel, cream
- Fit notes: define the waist with a belt or choose a blazer dress cut that still shows shape
- Why it works: combines power and softness while staying minimal
- Budget-friendly version: oversized blazer over a simple slip or mini dress with tall boots

#### 3. Silk button-up + tailored trousers + pointed-toe flats

- Best occasion: office, date, drinks, dinner
- Colors: cream, stone, navy, charcoal
- Fit notes: the shirt should drape softly; trousers should create a clean line through the leg
- Why it works: a classic quiet-luxury pairing that feels polished without looking severe
- Budget-friendly version: silky matte blouse + tailored trousers + pointed flats

#### 4. Fitted knit or corset top + baggy denim + kitten heels

- Best occasion: casual date, daytime-to-evening transition, party-lite
- Colors: cream, black, stone, denim blue
- Fit notes: the top should define the waist to balance the looser bottom
- Why it works: contrast makes the silhouette look intentional instead of accidental
- Budget-friendly version: ribbed knit top + relaxed denim + slingbacks or flats

#### 5. Slip dress layered with blazer or sweater

- Best occasion: date night, dinner, soft evening setting
- Colors: black, champagne, sage, cream
- Fit notes: let the dress skim rather than cling; use the third piece to add polish
- Why it works: layering softens the dress and makes it feel more refined
- Budget-friendly version: matte slip dress + lightweight cardigan or blazer

#### 6. Maxi skirt + knit or top + boots

- Best occasion: everyday, office, date, modest styling
- Colors: cream, olive, charcoal, brown
- Fit notes: keep the waist clear so the long line stays elegant rather than heavy
- Why it works: texture and verticality help the look feel premium
- Budget-friendly version: simple maxi skirt + ribbed knit + sleek boots
