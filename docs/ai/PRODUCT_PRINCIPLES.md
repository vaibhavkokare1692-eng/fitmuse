# Product Principles

- FitMuse is brief-to-board outfit planning.
- No closet upload is required.
- Output complete outfit boards, not product grids.
- Explain why each board matches the user brief.
- Trust before monetization.
- Fashion must be visual.
- Mobile first.

## Never Fake

- No fake live pricing.
- No fake stock or availability.
- No fake affiliate claims.
- No active cart or checkout claims.
- No exact-product visual claims unless legally sourced and verified.

## Current Shopping Truth

- Real boards use manually curated retailer candidates.
- Prices and availability require manual verification before purchase.
- Sample boards are style examples, not personalized shopping claims.
